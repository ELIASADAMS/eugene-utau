01

What changes in RE?

The original 友人 remains the preserved baseline. RE is a new recording generation designed to broaden what the bank can do while keeping the recognizable Eugene identity.

Phonetic coverage

Expanded Japanese CVVC material, long vowels, glottal stops, vocal fry and additional consonant behaviour.

Breath & texture

Breath/exhale material and rougher vocal textures are planned as first-class parts of the bank.

Expression

Experimental extras include laugh, giggle, sob, cough, throat and gasp material for non-standard performance.

Future-ready

The same structure can continue into RE2 and later generations without deleting earlier releases.
