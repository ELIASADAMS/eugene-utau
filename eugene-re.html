友人RE

The same friend. A different voice. 友人RE is the expanding next-generation line of the Eugene voicebank project — built to add range, articulation and expression without replacing the original.

IN DEVELOPMENT · RECLIST / RECORDING PHASE
01

What is 友人RE?

友人RE is not a replacement character. It is the next revision line of 友人: a new recording and expression layer built around the recognizable identity of the original voice.

The project is designed to grow as a family — 友人, 友人RE, 友人RE2 and future revisions can coexist as preserved stages of the same voicebank rather than overwriting one another.

Each major revision can introduce new recordings, pitch or tone layers, expression material and improved compatibility while keeping the original release archived and reproducible.

02

Expanded recording plan

The current 友人RE reclist expands the Japanese CVVC core with additional phonetic and expressive material. It is intentionally broader than a simple re-recording.

Expression extras

The experimental end of the current RE reclist includes dedicated material for non-lexical sounds. The planned set currently lists laugh 1, laugh 2, giggle, sob, cough 1, cough 2, throat, and gasp.
