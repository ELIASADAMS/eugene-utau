About the character

友人 is associated with memory, disappearance, unfinished things, and the feeling of returning from somewhere that cannot be named. He is quiet rather than theatrical: something familiar that survived when it should have disappeared.

The voice carries that idea. It was built from preserved fragments, rebuilt phonemes and post-production, producing a lived-in timbre that feels human, fragile and slightly distant.

“Preservation can be an act of love.”
04

Character notes

  • Possums, old Vocaloid scenes and eerie early VocaPi tracks.
  • Small obsessions, niche voices and haunting music.
  • Quiet time and things that are weird or unpolished.
  • Dislikes forced cheerfulness, pressure, shallow positivity and being treated as though he should be “normal”.
  • The small horns are part of Eugene's identity and function as a symbol of defiance in the character concept.
