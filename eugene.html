About 友人

友人 (Eugene) is a non-binary UTAU character using he/him pronouns. His presence is associated with memory, disappearance, unfinished things, and the feeling of someone returning from somewhere that cannot be named.

The voice was built from a carefully preserved collection of remnants, rebuilt phonemes, and extensive post-production. It has a lived-in quality: warm and gentle, but never completely clean or safe. There is always a trace of distance, as though Eugene is still trying to remember the shape of his own voice.

He does not demand attention. He arrives more like an echo — something familiar that survived when it should have disappeared.
