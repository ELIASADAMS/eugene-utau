01

The archive principle

Not a replacement

“New” does not automatically mean “better for every song”. Different recordings can have different artistic uses.

Document the state

Each major generation records what it is, what stage it reached, and what makes it different.

Keep the lineage

All generations remain visibly connected to 友人, rather than becoming unrelated banks with similar names.

Leave room

The site architecture reserves space for future voices, expressions, tools and media without another redesign.
