Welcome

Have you ever felt a voice came from the afterworld and sounded as though it had returned from somewhere no one can name?

友人 (Eugene) is a Japanese CVVC UTAU voicebank for UTAU and OpenUtau, created by Ilya Minin (Eli). The voice has a warm, intimate tone with a slightly uncanny edge, and works especially well for melancholic, lyrical, dark pop, industrial, and experimental music.
