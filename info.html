02

How generations work

Preserve

A released generation is not overwritten when a better recording is made. Its files, identity and documentation remain part of the archive.

Expand

A new generation may add pitches, aliases, phonetic coverage, breaths, textures, expressions or a different recording approach.

Compare

Each generation can be evaluated on its own terms. The site will eventually provide side-by-side technical and listening information.

Continue

RE2 and later revisions can be introduced without forcing users to abandon earlier banks or rewriting the history of the project.
