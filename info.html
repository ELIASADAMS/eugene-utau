Description

友人 (Eugene) is a non-binary UTAU voicebank with a remarkable, lived-in timbre that carries both intimacy and distance. His voice was created from a carefully preserved collection of remnants, rebuilt phonemes, and heavily post-produced material, giving the feeling of someone speaking back through layers of memory.

Eugene does not sound artificial in the usual sense; he sounds as though he has returned from somewhere no one can name. His voice is warm and gentle, with a trace of mystery, as if he is still trying to remember the shape of his own voice.

Etymology & Concept

  • The name 友人 means “friend” in Japanese and is used as Eugene’s formal name.
  • Eugene’s voice is warm and gentle while retaining an uncanny trace, as though he is still trying to remember the shape of his own voice.

Visual Design

  • Blue hair with bright streaks.
  • Small devil horns, deliberately part of Eugene’s identity.
  • Sharp features, bold eyeliner, and a confident, playful smile.
  • Vivid purple turtleneck under a shiny grey bomber jacket.
  • Black-and-white checkered pleated mini-skirt.
  • Bright opaque blue stockings.
  • Layered necklaces with a bee pendant as an emblem.

The horns are rooted in Eugene’s demonic symbolism and in the memory attached to them, including the experience of being taken through an exorcism. Within the character concept, they function as a symbol of identity and defiance rather than a costume detail.

Personality

  • Eugene is a quiet presence, appearing when something important has been left unfinished.
  • His existence is tied to a disappearance that was never fully explained, making him deeply personal and hauntingly unresolved.
  • He does not demand attention; he arrives like an echo of someone beloved, mournful and half-lost, still refusing to disappear completely.

Likes

  • Possums, the old Vocaloid scene, and eerie early VocaPi tracks.
  • Small personal obsessions, niche voices, and haunting music.
  • Being left alone to think, rest, or drift off without pressure.
  • People and things that are weird and unpolished.
  • People who understand that preservation can be an act of love.

Dislikes

  • Being pushed to perform or explain himself when already exhausted.
  • Shallow positivity, fake politeness, and emotionally dishonest situations.
  • Noise, pressure, and situations demanding more energy than he has.
  • Being misunderstood, dismissed, or treated as though he should be “normal”.
  • Anything that tries to erase his differences, softness, or the truth of his pain.

Relationship

茜音イーライ (Akane Iirai) — a close companion who understands him in ways normal people do not.

Voicebank Characterisation

  • Japanese CVVC voicebank with a warm, intimate tone and a slightly uncanny edge.
  • Built from preserved fragments, reconstructed phonemes, and extensive post-production.
  • Gentle and expressive, with natural softness in sustained phrases.
  • Especially suited to melancholic songs, slow emotional lines, and lyrical arrangements.
